# Pearl (PRL) Mining Pool — AethelBlock

Mine Pearl with AethelBlock using PearlHash. Choose PPLNS to receive a share of rewards from blocks found by the pool, or SOLO to earn the reward when your miner finds a valid block, subject to the pool fee.

[Pearl setup guide and dashboard](https://www.aethelblock.com/pearl?lang=en) · [All AethelBlock mining pools](https://github.com/AethelBlock/aethelblock-mining-pools)

## Connection settings

| Mode | Stratum endpoint | Standard fee | Minimum payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PPLNS | `prl-pool.aethelblock.com:3333` | 1% | 1 PRL |
| SOLO | `prl-solo.aethelblock.com:3334` | 1% | 1 PRL |

## Connect your miner

1. Choose hardware and mining software that support PearlHash on Pearl.
2. Select PPLNS or SOLO and enter the matching endpoint. Use your public PRL receiving address and the worker and protocol settings from the linked setup guide.
3. Start mining and check for accepted shares in your miner log. Open the matching pool dashboard and look up your receiving address to monitor your worker.
